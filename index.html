What the numbers mean

Total return
How much the money grew or shrank over the whole period. ₹1,00,000 becoming ₹1,18,000 is +18%.
Yearly growth (CAGR)
The average growth per year, smoothed out. It lets you compare periods of different lengths.
Max drawdown
The worst fall from a high point before a new high was reached. If money went up to ₹1,10,000 and then dropped to ₹95,000, the drawdown is about −13.6%. It shows how painful the ride would have been.
Win rate
The share of finished trades that made money. A high win rate alone does not mean a good strategy, because a few big losses can outweigh many small wins.
Time invested
The share of days the money was in the market. The rest of the time it was in cash.
Buy and hold
The baseline: buy on the first day and never sell. A strategy only adds value if it beats this, or if it gives a similar return with a much smaller drawdown.
Interest on cash
Money that is out of the market earns this yearly rate, like a liquid fund. It is counted in calendar days, so weekends are handled correctly. The dashed "Cash only" line shows what leaving everything in cash would have given. The rate is your own assumption, not a guarantee, and Bitcoin prices are in dollars, so use a rate that suits the data. Leave it at 0 for no interest.
Start and end date
The test uses only the days between these dates. The rule's averages are still worked out from the earlier data, so they are ready on the first day. If the rule is already in a buy state on your start date, it buys on the second day, just like buy and hold. A date with no data moves to the nearest day that has data. Try several start dates, because buy and hold depends heavily on when you begin.
Overfitting
Finding settings that look great on past data only because you tried many of them. Pick settings on the first 70%, then check them once on the last 30%. If the result falls apart there, the rule was probably fitted to the past.

Results come from past prices only. They do not predict the future, and this tool is for learning, not investment advice.
